What is a Nymserver?

A nymserver provides you with a persistent, two-way anonymous email address:

  • Your address: <alias>@nymserv-ng.virebent.art
  • You can send email to any clearnet recipient worldwide.
  • Anyone on the internet can send email to your alias.
  • Inbound email is encrypted to your public key and delivered back to you anonymously via the remailer mixnet (Reply-Block / YAMN).
  • The nymserver never knows your IP address, physical location, or real identity.

1. Legacy Protocol (crooks/ghionym compatible)

If you use manual CLI scripts, GnuPG, and Mixmaster reply blocks, nymserv-ng works right out of the box with zero changes to your existing workflow.

How to Register an Alias (Config)

Compose a plain text file, encrypt it with GnuPG for the nymserver public key, and send it via YAMN to:
config@nymserv-ng.virebent.art

Config:
From: youralias@nymserv-ng.virebent.art
Public-Key:
-----BEGIN PGP PUBLIC KEY BLOCK-----
... your public key here ...
-----END PGP PUBLIC KEY BLOCK-----
Reply-Block:
::
Anon-To: your_mailbox@example.org
... Mixmaster / Remailer chain ...

Nym-Commands: +cryptrecv +acksend +sigsend

Supported Account Flags

  • +cryptrecv: Encrypt incoming clearnet mail with your public key before delivery (default enabled, strongly recommended).
  • +sigsend: Require a valid PGP digital signature on all outgoing messages sent through your alias.
  • +acksend: Send an encrypted receipt back to your reply block when an outgoing mail is dispatched.
  • +fixedsz: Pad messages to fixed size units to frustrate packet length analysis.

How to Send Mail as Your Alias

Compose your message, sign it with your private PGP key, encrypt for the nymserver key, and send via YAMN to:
send@nymserv-ng.virebent.art

From: youralias@nymserv-ng.virebent.art
To: recipient@destination.org
Subject: Hello from the mixnet

This message was dispatched through nymserv-ng.

2. Next-Generation Protocol (Post-Quantum NG)

While the legacy protocol remains fully supported, OpenPGP and static RSA/ECC keys lack Forward Secrecy and are vulnerable to future quantum cryptanalysis (Shor's algorithm). nymserv-ng NG solves this:

  • Hybrid Post-Quantum KEM: NIST FIPS 203 ML-KEM-768 combined with X25519.
  • Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS): Ephemeral keys generated per message. Past traffic cannot be retroactively decrypted (Harvest Now, Decrypt Later immune).
  • Authenticated AEAD: ChaCha20-Poly1305 with zero ciphertext malleability.
  • Kernel Memory Hygiene: Master keys locked in physical RAM (mlock) with guard pages and deterministic memory wiping on drop.
